FAQs

Questions Huron County Homeowners Ask Us

From lake-effect cold to propane tanks and century-home ductwork, here are straight answers to the questions we hear most across the county.

How fast can you get to a furnace outage in rural Huron County?

Because we are based in the county, our technicians reach rural properties around Hensall, Blyth, and Wingham faster than a crew routed from the city. We prioritise no-heat calls, and on most winter outages a tech is on the way the same day, parts in hand for the common propane, oil, and gas furnaces.

Do you service propane and oil furnaces, or only natural gas?

All three. A large share of Huron County homes outside the towns run propane or oil heat, and our technicians service those systems every week alongside the natural gas units common in Goderich, Clinton, and Exeter. We carry the parts and the training for each fuel type.

Can you replace my oil furnace with a heat pump?

Yes. We install cold-climate heat pumps that take over the full winter heating load, remove the old oil tank, and walk you through the available rebate paperwork. Today's units keep heating well below zero, so lake-wind nights are not a problem.

We own a century home. Can you handle older ductwork and equipment?

Absolutely. Heritage homes are a big part of what we do in places like Bayfield, Blyth, and Seaforth. We are comfortable working with aging duct runs, retrofitted systems, oil and propane setups, and the quirks that come with a house built a hundred years ago.

Do you install mini-splits in farm workshops and outbuildings?

We do. Cold-climate mini-splits are a popular add for Huron County farms, heating and cooling workshops, milk houses, and converted barns without needing ductwork. We size the unit to the space and mount the outdoor pad level so it lasts through the winter.

Which towns across Huron County do you cover?

All of them. We serve Goderich, Clinton, Exeter, Seaforth, Wingham, Bayfield, Blyth, Hensall, and the rural properties in between. If you are inside Huron County, you are inside our service area.

Can you add backup power for winter outages?

Yes. As a Generac dealer we sell, install, and service standby generators that keep your furnace, well pump, and lights on through lake-effect storms and rural outages across Huron County.
